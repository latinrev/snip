# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Win+Shift+S opens a frozen capture screen with Screenshot, Scrolling and Video modes. Print Screen does the same, and can be turned off.
- Screenshots: drag an area, click a window, or press Enter for the monitor. Copied to the clipboard and saved to Pictures\Screenshots as PNG.
- Scrolling capture: scrolls a page from top to bottom and stitches it into one full-resolution image, keeping sticky headers and footers once and trimming the scrollbar.
- Screen recording: H.264 MP4 at 30 fps to Videos\Screen Recordings, with click-to-zoom that follows the cursor. Stop with Win+Shift+S or the Stop button.
- Win+Shift+A: history of the last 24 hours of screenshots, loaded as you scroll. Picking one copies it and pastes it into the previous app.
- Starts with Windows, single instance, per-monitor DPI aware, no network access. Needs 64-bit Windows 10 version 2004 or later.
